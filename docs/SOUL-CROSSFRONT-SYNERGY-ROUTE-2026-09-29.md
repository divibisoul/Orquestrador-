## Architectural rule

The sequence is a composition route, not a replacement topology.

Adjacent direct fusion remains governed by the existing adjacent-only rule.
Non-adjacent stages are delegated through the canonical Mesh or the N07→SARA proxy.
No nucleus loses its native authority.

## Proof states

DECLARED = route exists as architecture.
CONFIGURED = transports/credentials are available.
CONNECTED = real stage invocation returned with original correlation.
VERIFIED = every required stage has real correlated evidence and the composite result is traceable.

A route must never be promoted from DECLARED/CONFIGURED to VERIFIED merely because
the route definition exists.
